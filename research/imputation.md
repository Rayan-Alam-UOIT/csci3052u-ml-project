URIEL+'s imputation pipeline uses family information in the BFS-based "genetic imputation" step in `aggregate()`, which relies on `get_dialects()` to build a parent -> child map from `dialects.csv` (keyed by ISO 639-3 or Glottocode) and then propagates known feature values down the tree from root languages to their dialectal descendants. This is a simple presence or absence tree-walk, not a distance- or lineage-weighted method, and it happens before (not inside) the statistical imputers. The actual statistical imputation strategies (such as KNN, SoftImpute, MIDAS, and mean) operate solely on the typological and script feature matrices (S_, P_, INV_, M_, SC_ columns) and never take family, lineage, or genealogical distance as an input feature. This means that any richer genealogical signal (like the full lineage strings in `lang_fam_geo.csv`) is not used by them at all. Geographic or location data, such as the latitude and longitude fields present in `lang_fam_geo.csv`, are not referenced anywhere in this imputation code. There is no areal or coordinate-based feature or distance metric feeding into any of the imputers. In short, family info is used, but only narrowly (as a tree-propagation heuristic prior to statistical imputation), and location info is not used at all, despite both being available in the URIEL+ data CSVs.
